## The problem

I spotted a market gap: small apartments have wall height to spare but little storage that is easy to reach.

## My approach

I led iterative design in Fusion 360 of a rolling-rail, magnetic-latch mechanism and validated it to a 15 lb load. After design-for-assembly testing, I redesigned the hinge and pin features to eliminate misassembly and make the build more reliable.

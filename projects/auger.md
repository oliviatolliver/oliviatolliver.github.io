## The problem

The previous scoop design could only reach shallow soil and gathered small samples each cycle. The new system also had to stay under a 5 kg weight limit.

## My approach

I designed the vertical translation assembly around an 18-in non-captive lead screw, dual 0.5-in hardened steel guide rods, and linear ball bearings to improve stability and reduce applied moments. I selected a Maxon EC-Flat 45 (70 W) motor with a 50:1 gearbox for 5.7 Nm of torque, then tested it to show 150 to 300 N of vertical load capacity, enough to support the 110 lb rover under stall.

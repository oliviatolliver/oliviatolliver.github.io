## The problem

Low-power wearables such as EKG sensors need a way to generate their own power from the motion of the body.

## My approach

I designed, fabricated, and tested three pneumatic pouch geometries, using data acquisition and MATLAB to find the best performer. I also developed a foam insole mechanism that converts foot-strike pressure into airflow to drive a 2-in axial-flux generator.

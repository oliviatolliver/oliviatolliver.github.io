## The problem

Retail shade systems offer limited ranges and often miss on uneven complexions, so many people never find a true match.

## My approach

I am engineering the pigment cartridge, the automated dispensing, and the mixing mechanisms, using SolidWorks, DFMA, and microcontroller-driven controls to bring personalized shade matching into a consumer device.

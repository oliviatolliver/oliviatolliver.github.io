About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Cornell, drawn to product design and robotics: building things people can actually use, from first sketch to working prototype.

Outside of class I lead a mechanical subteam on the Cornell Mars Rover team, run lab sections as a teaching assistant, and plan conference programming for 55 NSBE members.
